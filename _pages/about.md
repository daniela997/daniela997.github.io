Hi! I am a Research Scientist at the [Centre for Data Science & AI](https://www.gla.ac.uk/research/az/datascienceai/) at the University of Glasgow, collaborating on interdisciplinary machine learning research across all colleges. My research background covers repurposing multimodal generative models for visual understanding tasks such as segmentation, damage detection, 3D representation, and more recently for fine-grained plankton classification and biodiversity monitoring. I completed my PhD in the [Computer Vision and Autonomous Systems (CVAS)](https://www.gla.ac.uk/schools/computing/research/researchsections/ida-section/computervisionandautonomoussystems/) research group, supervised by [Dr John Williamson](http://johnhw.com/) and [Dr Paul Henderson](http://www.pmh47.net/).  

Prior to my PhD, I completed an Undergraduate degree in Computing Science at the University of Glasgow with Honours of the First Class. My final year project, "A Deep Learning approach to artefact correction in photographic film" combined my newly developed interested in Machine Learning with my passion for analogue film photography, and served as a foundation for my PhD proposal. This work set me off onto my research journey into developing machine learning approaches in various interdisciplinary contexts, such as cultural heritage, medical and underwater imaging. Apart from that, I worked in large-format printing for two years during the start of my degree, and as a Full-stack Software Engineer during my Honours.
